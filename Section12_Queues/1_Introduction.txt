Queue is a logical data structure. It works on discipline FIFO (First in first out)
Queue will have two ends, called as front end, and rear end. 

Data:
    1, Space for storing elements
    2, Front - for deletion
    3. Rear - for Insertion

Operation:
    1. enqueue(x)
    2. dequeue()
    3. isEmpty()
    4. isFull()
    5. first()
    6. last()
