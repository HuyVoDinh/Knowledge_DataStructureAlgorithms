Traversing means visiting all the elements or visiting all the nodes. 
If a data structure is linear, can traverse in 2 methods.
    + First to last, that is forward, 
    + Last to first, backward.

In nonlinear:
    + Preorder: visit(node), Preorder(left subtree), Preorder(right subtree): Means visiting a node first, visiting any node, then performing preorder on its left subtree and performing preorder on its right subtree. 
    + Inorder: Inorder (left), visit (node), Inorder (right): perform inorder traversal on left subtree, then visit a node, then perform inorder traversal on right subtree.
    + Postorder: Postorder (left), Postorder (right), visit (node): Perform postorder on left subtree, right subtree, then visit a node.
    + Levelorder: Level by level: Visit all the nodes level by level.
